# Stream harness results — 2026-10-30 (EliteMini, Radeon 780M, GNOME seat 2560x1440 @2x)

Unattended: headless Chromium through `netem.py`, `ab.py` / `soak.py`. Profiles: hotel (45 Mbps, 20 ms + 0-70 ms
jitter, 3 s collapse to 1 Mbps every 60 s), office (100 Mbps, 5 ms + 0-2 ms). Sharpness = Laplacian variance of
a text screenshot (higher = crisper). Latency = key press -> first decoded frame showing the change.

## Harness findings fixed along the way

- First session on a freshly restarted selkies sometimes went 15 s frameless (agent watchdog restart): 5/5 such
  stalls were first-run-after-restart; `ab.py` now discards a warm-up run. Real sessions: 2 watchdog restarts in a week.
- selkies re-writes its last frame ~1.2 s when idle; frames are counted by timestamp.
- Firefox first-run dialog ate input; hidden `#videoCanvas`; F9 toggle state carried between runs (now F9/F10).
- Open: the outage animation occasionally fails to start (1 of 4 runs, voided by the motion check).
