# Use Styx

For people who have an account on a Styx portal and want to get work done: sign in, open a desktop, connect to a workstation, or launch a proxied app.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **[Getting started](getting-started.md)**

    Sign in, set up the sign-in card (2FA), use SSO, change your password.

-   **[Desktops](desktops.md)**

    Launch a containerized desktop from a template, keep it awake, stop it.

-   **[Workstations](workstations.md)**

    Stream a physical Linux machine: panel, clipboard, files, audio, scaling.

-   **[Apps and the shell](services.md)**

    Open Jellyfin, Sonarr, Home Assistant and friends through one hostname.

-   **[Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md)**

    Black screen, 502, offline machine, locked account.

</div>
